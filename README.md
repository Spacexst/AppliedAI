\# Applied AI



This repository contains projects and coursework developed as part of my Applied AI studies.



\## Project



\### Maternal Health Risk Classification



The project explores the use of machine learning to classify maternal health risk into three categories:



\- Low Risk

\- Mid Risk

\- High Risk



The model uses maternal health measurements such as:



\- Age

\- Systolic Blood Pressure

\- Diastolic Blood Pressure

\- Blood Sugar

\- Body Temperature

\- Heart Rate



\## Dataset



The project uses the Maternal Health Risk dataset available through the UCI Machine Learning Repository.



Dataset source:

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/
